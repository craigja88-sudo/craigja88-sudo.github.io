Dumpster for a whole-house cleanout

A whole-house or estate cleanout is the job 20-yard dumpsters were made for. Light contents can fit a 15. Furniture plus attic plus garage often wants a 20 or a 30 — or a second haul, which is sometimes cheaper than overfilling.

Walk the house. Books, wet carpet, and plaster are heavy. Hollow furniture is bulky. Those two facts fight: a box can look half empty and still be over the tonnage cap, or look full of sofas and still be under weight. Tell the hauler it is mixed household, not construction, so you get the right ticket.

Starting size

  • Apartment or sparse house: 15-yard.
  • Typical house cleanout: 20-yard.
  • Estate, basement, garage, and attic in one weekend: 30-yard, or a 20 with a planned swap.

A second haul of a 20-yard is often cleaner than cramming a 40-yard onto a short driveway the truck cannot enter. Fit and weight beat a bigger number on the quote.

Paint, chemicals, batteries, tires, and refrigerant appliances stay out unless the hauler’s list says otherwise. Food waste and mixed wet debris weigh more after a rain — cover the load if you can. Chooser. Placement.
