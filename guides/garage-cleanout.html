Project guide

Dumpster for a garage cleanout

A packed two-car garage is usually a 15- or 20-yard dumpster. The surprise is never the broken chair. It is the paint, batteries, tires, and the wet cardboard that blows the weight cap.

Household junk looks light until it is wet, stacked, and mixed with lumber offcuts. Walk the garage once before you book. Separate anything the landfill will reject: oil-based paint, solvents, batteries, tires, and appliances with refrigerant. The hauler’s prohibited list wins. We will not invent a local exception.

Starting size

  • One-car or a light cleanout: 10-yard if you are honest about the pile.
  • Typical two-car packed garage: 15-yard, 20 if you are also doing the shed or attic.
  • Estate, basement plus garage: 20-yard, sometimes 30. See whole-house cleanout.

HOAs often care more about a garage-driveway box than you think — get the duration in writing. Private driveway is still the simpler permit path; driveway vs street.

Do not mix dirt, concrete, or a leftover patio tear-out into a household-junk dumpster unless the quote says so. That mix is how a cheap 20-yard becomes an overage. Size guide.
