How it works

  1. Pick the city. Local notes on climate, construction, and where to ask about permits — not a fake fee table.
  2. Pick a size. 10, 15, 20, 30, or 40 cubic yards. Most home jobs are a 10 or a 20.
  3. Request quotes. Tell haulers the ZIP, debris type, and whether the box sits on a driveway or in the street.
  4. Compare weight and days, not just the headline price. Overage and extra haul-aways are how cheap quotes get expensive.

Quick answers

What size dumpster do I need?

Most single-room remodels fit a 10-yard. Roofs and whole-house cleanouts usually want a 20. Heavy debris (concrete, dirt, roofing) is limited by weight, so a smaller box is often required. Use the chooser for a starting point.

Do I need a permit?

Driveway: often no city street permit. Street or sidewalk: usually yes. Confirm with your city. We do not publish invented municipal fees. Permit notes.

What does it cost?

Plan for the mid hundreds of dollars for a week in many US cities, more for large boxes, long hauls, or heavy debris. See honest ranges — not a fake $99 teaser.
