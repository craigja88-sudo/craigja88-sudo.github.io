Quick answers

What size dumpster do I need?

Most single-room remodels fit a 10-yard. Roofs and whole-house cleanouts usually want a 20. Heavy debris (concrete, dirt, roofing) is limited by weight, so a smaller box is often required. Use the chooser or a project guide.

Do I need a permit?

Driveway: often no city street permit. Street or sidewalk: usually yes. Confirm with your city. We do not publish invented municipal fees. Permit notes.

What does it cost?

Plan for the mid hundreds of dollars for a week in many US cities, more for large boxes, long hauls, or heavy debris. See honest ranges — not a fake $99 teaser.
