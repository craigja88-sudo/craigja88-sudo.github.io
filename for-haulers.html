For haulers

Got trucks? Get listed.

Which Dumpster is a directory for people who typed “[city] dumpster rental.” We would rather send them to independent roll-off companies than to a nameless national form forever.

If you own or dispatch roll-off dumpsters and want to appear on a city page — or take referral leads when quote matching goes live — email Jordan Craig: craigja88@gmail.com.

What to include in the email

  • Company name and the cities you actually service (not a 200-mile wish radius).
  • Sizes you drop (10 / 15 / 20 / 30 / 40).
  • Whether you will pull street permits.
  • A phone or booking email you want published. We will not list you until that is real.

We do not invent competitors or scrape fake reviews onto your city page. If a market says “Coming soon,” it is empty on purpose.
