For haulers

Got trucks? Get listed.

Dumpster Directory is a directory for people who typed a city plus dumpster rental. We would rather send them to independent roll-off companies than to a nameless national form forever.

Email Jordan Craig: craigja88@gmail.com.

How listing works

  • Featured listing Verified companies get a named listing on the city pages they actually service — phone or booking email you approve, sizes you drop, whether you pull street permits. Until then the city page stays “Coming soon.” We do not invent competitors.
  • Finder’s fee on completed jobs If we send a customer and the rental actually runs, we invoice a finder’s fee on that completed job. Not on every inbound email. Not on quotes that never drop a box. Terms in writing before the first lead.
  • 60-day out Either side can end the listing arrangement with 60 days’ notice. No lock-in beyond that. Take your phone number with you.

What to include in the email

  • Company name and the cities you actually service (not a 200-mile wish radius).
  • Sizes you drop (10 / 15 / 20 / 30 / 40).
  • Whether you will pull street permits.
  • A phone or booking email you want published. We will not list you until that is real.
