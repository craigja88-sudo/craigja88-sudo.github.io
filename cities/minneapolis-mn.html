Minneapolis, Minnesota

Dumpster rental in Minneapolis

Minneapolis dumpsters deal with Twin Cities winters, older housing stock, and a city-vs-suburb split across Hennepin County.

Snow and freeze-thaw are scheduling facts here. Remodels of early-1900s homes and duplexes throw off heavy debris. The City of Minneapolis is one desk; St. Paul is across the river in Ramsey County, and first-ring suburbs (Bloomington, Edina, St. Louis Park, and the rest) each run their own rules.

Many older blocks have alleys. We will not invent a dollar fee.

Sizes that actually get used

Same US sizes as everywhere: 10, 15, 20, 30, and 40 cubic yards. Most Minneapolis home projects land on a 10-yard (bathroom, plaster) or a 20-yard (roof, cleanout, kitchen-plus). Winter roofing still happens; ice on the drive is a truck problem. Size guide and chooser. Project notes: roof, kitchen, cleanout, concrete.

Pricing recap

Winter extra days and short daylight windows are the local adders. National context: many 10-yard weeks quote around $250–$500 and 20-yard weeks around $350–$700 before overage, extra days, and trip fees. Not a Minneapolis guarantee. What changes the bill.
