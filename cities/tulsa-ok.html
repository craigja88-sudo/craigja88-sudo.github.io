Tulsa, Oklahoma

Dumpster rental in Tulsa

Tulsa dumpsters deal with Green Country heat, spring storm weeks, and a city-vs-suburb permit split across Tulsa County.

Hot, humid summers and spring storms are scheduling facts here. Hail and wind send roofing debris out in bunches. The City of Tulsa is one desk; Broken Arrow, Jenks, Bixby, Owasso, and Sand Springs each run their own rules. A Tulsa permit does not cover a suburban drop.

Midtown lots can be tight. Confirm driveway vs street before the truck shows. We will not invent a dollar fee.

Sizes that actually get used

Same US sizes as everywhere: 10, 15, 20, 30, and 40 cubic yards. Most Tulsa home projects land on a 10-yard (bathroom, plaster) or a 20-yard (roof, cleanout, kitchen-plus). After a storm, roofing volume jumps; weight still matters more than the yard number. Size guide and chooser. Project notes: roof, kitchen, cleanout, concrete.

Pricing recap

Storm weeks and extra haul days are the local adders. National context: many 10-yard weeks quote around $250–$500 and 20-yard weeks around $350–$700 before overage, extra days, and trip fees. Not a Tulsa guarantee. What changes the bill.

Permits in Tulsa

City of Tulsa vs the suburb vs Tulsa County. General rule, still true here: a box entirely on a private driveway is often no street permit; public street, sidewalk, or other right-of-way usually is. The city treats a dumpster in the street as a right-of-way use. Confirm with the City of Tulsa, or with the suburb you actually sit in. Driveway vs street.

Do not assume a City of Tulsa permit covers Broken Arrow, Jenks, Bixby, Owasso, or Sand Springs. If the box has to sit in the street, ask the city before you assume that is allowed.
