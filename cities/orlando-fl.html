Orlando, Florida

Dumpster rental in Orlando

Orlando dumpsters serve suburban housing, HOA communities, and a commercial overlay of hospitality construction that homeowners should not price against.

Central Florida is hot, sandy, and in hurricane season June–November. Theme-park-adjacent traffic does not change your dumpster size; it can change delivery windows. The City of Orlando, unincorporated Orange County, and neighbors (Winter Park, Kissimmee, Sanford) each handle right-of-way differently. We will not publish a made-up permit charge.

HOAs are the gatekeeper in many planned communities. A driveway drop that the city ignores can still violate association covenants. Get it in writing.

Sizes that actually get used

Same US sizes as everywhere: 10, 15, 20, 30, and 40 cubic yards. Most Orlando home projects land on a 10-yard (bathroom, small deck, heavy debris) or a 20-yard (roof, cleanout, kitchen-plus). Size guide and chooser.

Pricing recap

Household quotes often sit in national ranges. Storm weeks and roofing season after hail or hurricanes are when availability, not a brochure price, sets the number. National context: many 10-yard weeks quote around $250–$500 and 20-yard weeks around $350–$700 before overage, extra days, and trip fees. Not a Orlando guarantee. What changes the bill.

Permits in Orlando

City of Orlando vs Orange County vs your suburb — match the office to the address. Street placement is the usual trigger for a permit; driveway is often private. General rule, still true here: private driveway is often no street permit; public street usually is. Driveway vs street.

Sandy soil and slab-on-grade homes: dirt and concrete still need a heavy-material dumpster, usually a smaller box. Do not toss paint and pool chemicals in with household junk; Central Florida landfills are not shy about rejecting loads.
