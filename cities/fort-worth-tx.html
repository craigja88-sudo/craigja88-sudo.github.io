Fort Worth, Texas

Dumpster rental in Fort Worth

Fort Worth is the western half of DFW — heat, new construction on the fringe, and a historic near-downtown that still uses tight streets.

Tarrant County, the City of Fort Worth, Arlington, and the mid-cities are separate. Summers are hot; winter ice storms are occasional but real for truck scheduling. New subdivisions west and north generate construction debris; older near-downtown lots can be access-limited.

HOAs in master-planned areas restrict placement and duration. We do not publish Fort Worth’s fee.

Sizes that actually get used

Same US sizes as everywhere: 10, 15, 20, 30, and 40 cubic yards. Most Fort Worth home projects land on a 10-yard (bathroom, heavy debris) or a 20-yard (roof, cleanout, kitchen-plus). New-build leftover lumber is bulky; leftover masonry is heavy — they are not the same box. Size guide and chooser. Project notes: roof, kitchen, cleanout, concrete.

Pricing recap

A competitive DFW market. Quotes often sit in the middle of national ranges unless you are far from a landfill or loading concrete. National context: many 10-yard weeks quote around $250–$500 and 20-yard weeks around $350–$700 before overage, extra days, and trip fees. Not a Fort Worth guarantee. What changes the bill.
