Permits

Permit resources, not legal advice

Dumpster Directory does not give legal advice. We do not tell you when you need a permit, what a city charges, or whether a driveway, street, alley, or sidewalk is public right-of-way. Those questions belong to the government that owns the pavement.

This is not legal advice. Permit rules, fees, and applications are set by cities and counties. They change. If a quote includes “permit,” ask the hauler whether that is the city’s charge or a filing fee — then confirm on the official site. We will not invent municipal fees, phone numbers, or government URLs.
