Privacy

Which Dumpster is a static dumpster-rental directory operated by Jordan Craig. Contact: craigja88@gmail.com. Last updated August 30, 2026.

What this site collects

The quote forms on this site do not send your answers to a server. They stay in the browser and show a follow-up message. If you email craigja88@gmail.com, we receive whatever you put in that message (usually a city, ZIP, size, dates, and a return address).

We use that email to answer you and, if you asked for quotes, to follow up about dumpster rental. We do not sell email lists. We do not run a live quote-matching backend yet.

What we do not collect today

  • No account system, no passwords.
  • No first-party analytics cookies, and no advertising pixels that we control.
  • No sale of personal information.

Third parties this page loads

Pages load IBM Plex from Google Fonts. Google may see your IP address and browser as they would on any site that uses their font CDN. We do not get a copy of that log.

If you click a mailto link, your own email app sends the message. That is between you and your mail provider.

Affiliates and haulers

We may earn a commission if you book a dumpster through this site. When quote matching or partner links go live, we may share the job details you gave us with a hauler or affiliate network so they can quote you. We will not share your address for unrelated marketing.

Partner payouts are logged privately. That ledger is not published on this site.

How long we keep email

We keep inquiry email long enough to answer it and, if a booking completes, long enough to pay or collect a finder fee. You can ask us to delete a thread: email craigja88@gmail.com from the same address and say so.

Changes

If we add analytics, a live form backend, or paid ads, this page will be updated before that data is collected. The date at the top of this page is the marker.
